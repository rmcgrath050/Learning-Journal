#### Three key measures!! 
- thoughput  : amount of data processed per second
- errors rates : Frequency of failures under load 
- latency : time to complete tasks 


<b> Why does testing and deployment matter so much and what is UAT doing? </b>

- Load testing: the expected size of the crowd, what you plan for and can serve
- Stress tesing: Delibrate overcrowding, testing possible system weak points
- Bottleneck : slowest link in your data flow, pipeline only runs as fast as slowest stage
